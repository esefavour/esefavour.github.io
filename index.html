It was never about the math.

After more than seventeen years in Nigerian public service, the author kept seeing the same thing: well-paid people ending their careers under unserviced debt.

The cause is rarely that they can’t add up. We borrow our wants from the people around us, a habit the philosopher René Girard called mimetic desire. Then credit and social media make keeping up easy to finance and hard to stop.

The result: a balance sheet full of things that lose value, and none of the things that pay you. This book gives that pattern a name, then gives you the accounting tools to break it.
